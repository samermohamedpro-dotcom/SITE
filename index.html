Our Story

She spends her days making sure no one around her is missing anything. Her children. Her home. Everyone else, first.

Baking is the one thing she does just for her.

People kept tasting it, and asking her to make it again. Family. Friends. Then friends of friends. So she stopped saying no.

Not a business plan. Not made to scale. Just something rare, made by someone who still finds an hour that belongs to no one but her.

Petit Gâteau
Our Ingredients

How We Source

What started in our home kitchen is still made there: small batches, by hand, with ingredients we'd happily put on our own table.

We hold every ingredient to the same standard: organic, high quality, nothing we'd hesitate to use ourselves. If we can't source it that way, we don't make it.

Ordering

Made to order, delivered to you.

Everything is baked at home the day it reaches you — nothing sits waiting on a shelf. There is no shop and no counter: we deliver across Qatar, and every order starts with a message.

Rare, on purpose: small batches only, so it's worth the wait.
